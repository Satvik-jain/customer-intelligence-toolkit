# Decision factors

Use these as candidate explanations, then connect each to evidence or a test.

| Lens | Questions to consider |
|---|---|
| Goal and motivation | What progress or outcome is the customer seeking in this situation? |
| Journey stage | Is the issue need recognition, search, evaluation, purchase, or post-purchase? Could the customer skip stages through habit? |
| Perceived risk and trust | What could go wrong, who bears the cost, and what proof or safeguards matter? |
| Emotion and attention | What emotional state or appeal may change attention or risk tolerance in this context? |
| Heuristics and reference points | What anchors, defaults, comparisons, scarcity cues, or social signals are present? |
| Social context | Who else influences, approves, uses, pays, or observes the decision? |
| Culture and setting | Which local norms, language, institutions, or situational goals may change interpretation? |

Avoid translating a theory into a claim about an individual or population without evidence. Prefer conditional hypotheses and observable implications.
