# Market framework selection

Choose tools by the question being answered.

| Question | Useful lens | What it cannot establish alone |
|---|---|---|
| What pressures shape industry economics? | Five Forces | Current customer demand, internal capability, or a forecast |
| What external changes could affect the market? | PESTLE or scenario scan | Probability or business impact without evidence |
| How does the organization compare internally and externally? | SWOT/TOWS, supplemented by capability evidence | Priorities or strategy if left as an unranked list |
| Which competitors are closest? | Strategic groups or competitor map | A uniquely correct market structure or customer preference |
| How large is the opportunity? | Bottom-up TAM/SAM/SOM with explicit assumptions | A forecast or achievable share without capacity and conversion evidence |
| Where is execution performance different? | Benchmarking | Causality or a fair comparison when measures are not comparable |

Triangulate frameworks with current public or licensed sources and customer evidence. Record market boundaries, definitions, source dates, comparable metrics, and assumptions. Revisit scans when material conditions change.

## Source note

Five Forces, PESTLE guidance, and TOWS have identifiable sources; the framework-selection table and its cautions are this toolkit's operational synthesis. The source set does not establish a definitive origin for SWOT. See [verified source notes](../../../references/sources.md#strategy-and-market-frameworks).
