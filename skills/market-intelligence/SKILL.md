# Market Intelligence

Use this skill to understand the external market and competitive context for a specific business decision.

## Inputs

Collect the offering, customer/job, geography, time horizon, decision, known competitors or alternatives, and accessible sources. Clarify whether the user needs current market facts or a framework-based analysis.

## Workflow

1. Define the market boundary, customer need, geography, time period, and relevant alternatives.
2. Separate direct competitors, indirect competitors, substitutes, and potential entrants.
3. Select only the frameworks that answer the question. Use the [framework-selection reference](references/framework-selection.md) to avoid framework checklists without a decision purpose.
4. Assess market structure, buyer/supplier power, rivalry, substitutes, entry barriers, macro factors, and strategic groups as relevant.
5. If sizing is requested, make each bottom-up assumption explicit and use top-down estimates only as a cross-check. Distinguish TAM, SAM, and SOM.
6. Record source provenance and dates. Use public or licensed information and do not imply access to private competitor data.
7. Summarize implications, open questions, and what should be monitored over time.

## Output

Provide scope, competitor/alternative map, selected framework findings, sizing logic if applicable, source list, assumptions, confidence, and strategic implications. Use the shared [evidence labels](../../references/evidence-labels.md).

## Limits

Frameworks such as Five Forces and SWOT are not forecasts and do not quantify market outcomes by themselves. Do not inflate market sizes, treat a map as measured customer preference, or present dated claims as current. If current evidence cannot be accessed, say so and provide a research plan.
