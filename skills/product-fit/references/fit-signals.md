# Product-fit signal review

Review several independent signal families. Avoid a single-score verdict.

| Signal family | Examples of useful evidence | Common caution |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | Frequency, urgency, workaround, consequence | Stated pain may not imply priority or payment |
| Solution value | Task outcome, perceived benefits and sacrifices | Satisfaction can be high in a biased sample |
| Usability and adoption | Task success, friction, activation, time to value | A usable product can still lack demand |
| Retention and behavior | Repeat use, renewal, cohort retention, repeat purchase | Incentives, contract lock-in, and seasonality affect retention |
| Monetization | Payment, renewal, expansion, price tests | Survey intent and simulation do not establish actual WTP |
| Alternatives | Why customers switch, stay, or choose a workaround | Feature comparisons may miss trust, access, and switching costs |

Interpret the signals for a defined segment, time period, and business model. Treat heuristic thresholds as prompts for investigation, not universal proof of fit.

## Source note

The signal families are a project-authored synthesis, not a standardized product-market-fit score or validated scale. Evaluate each signal using a method appropriate to the claim and preserve its limitations.
