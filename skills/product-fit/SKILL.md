# Product Fit

Use this skill to evaluate evidence that a product solves an important customer problem and continues to deliver value.

## Inputs

Identify the target segment, job or problem, product stage, alternatives, customer evidence, usage or retention data, and the decision at hand.

## Workflow

1. Define the target segment and problem context; distinguish problem-solution fit from a scaling or market-size question.
2. Assess problem importance, frequency, current workaround, and consequences using customer evidence.
3. Assess solution value relative to price, time, effort, risk, and available alternatives.
4. Review usability and adoption barriers separately from demand.
5. Examine behavioral signals such as activation, repeat use, retention, renewal, referral, and payment where available. Check cohort, selection, and measurement limitations.
6. Compare qualitative explanations with observed behavior and competitive alternatives.
7. Identify gaps and recommend the next validation step. Treat PMF as an evolving, multi-signal judgment rather than a single threshold.

## Output

Summarize fit evidence by problem, solution value, usability/adoption, retention/behavior, economics, and alternatives. Separate positive evidence, counterevidence, assumptions, and next tests. Use the shared [evidence labels](../../references/evidence-labels.md).

## Limits

Do not claim PMF from synthetic panels, survey enthusiasm, one metric, a rule of thumb, or growth without retention. Do not infer causality from correlations. If sample or cohort details are missing, state that the signal is not interpretable enough for a strong conclusion.
