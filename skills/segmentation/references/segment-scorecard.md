# Segment scorecard

Score each candidate relative to the decision and available evidence; do not invent numeric scores.

| Criterion | Questions |
|---|---|
| Need distinction | Is there a materially different job, constraint, or desired outcome? |
| Identifiability | Can customers be recognized using available, ethical, measurable information? |
| Reachability | Can the team access and serve the group through realistic channels? |
| Responsiveness | Is there reason to expect a distinct response to the product or offer? |
| Attractiveness | Is the opportunity large or valuable enough for this decision? State the evidence and assumptions. |
| Strategic fit | Can the organization meet the group’s needs and support the economics? |
| Stability | Are the needs durable enough, or should the segment be treated as temporary? |
| Fairness and privacy | Does the definition use sensitive data or create exclusion, stereotyping, or intrusive targeting? |

For each segment, report its operational definition, supporting observations, unknowns, and a way to validate whether it is distinct. Personas add context; they do not replace segment criteria.

## Source note

Segmentation has an established marketing literature, and measurability, accessibility, differentiability, actionability, and related criteria appear in marketing guidance. The full scorecard and its additional criteria are this toolkit's operational synthesis, not a validated scale.
