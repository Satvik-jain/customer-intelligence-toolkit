# Positioning and message checks

## Positioning statement

For **[target segment]** who need **[job or outcome]**, **[offering]** is a **[category/frame]** that **[distinct customer benefit]**, supported by **[credible reason to believe]**.

## Review checks

- The target and competitive frame are understandable.
- Category expectations or points of parity are credible.
- The differentiating benefit matters to the target and is supported by the product.
- Proof is available and traceable; claims are not stronger than evidence.
- The language describes customer outcomes before implementation detail.
- The message fits the channel and decision stage.

## Test plan prompts

Compare message variants with a defined customer group. Choose a measure appropriate to the decision: comprehension, recall, qualified interest, task choice, or actual conversion. Predefine the primary outcome and guardrails. A synthetic response can surface ambiguous wording, but it cannot estimate real preference or conversion.

## Source note

The target / frame / value / proof statement is this toolkit's operational template, informed by published practitioner formats; it is not a universal canonical formula or validated instrument. The parity and differentiation checks draw on established positioning guidance. See [verified source notes](../../../references/sources.md#segmentation-and-positioning).
