# Customer Validation

Use this skill to reduce uncertainty around a decision using the method best suited to the question.

## Inputs

Require the decision, hypothesis or uncertainty, target population, current evidence, baseline if available, practical constraints, and the cost of a wrong decision.

## Workflow

1. State the decision and make the hypothesis falsifiable.
2. Identify what the decision needs to know: whether a problem exists, why it occurs, whether a design is usable, how common a preference is, what trade-off customers make, or whether an intervention changes behavior.
3. Choose a fit-for-question method. Use interviews/observation for discovery, task testing for usability, surveys for stated responses, conjoint for attribute trade-offs, and randomized or carefully designed field tests for causal impact. See [method selection](references/method-selection.md).
4. Define population, recruitment, sampling limitations, primary outcome, guardrails, smallest useful effect, instrumentation, and analysis plan before collection.
5. Plan sample size or power only when the necessary baseline, variance, effect threshold, and design are available. Avoid defaulting to universal thresholds.
6. Report execution issues, effect estimates, uncertainty, practical importance, and alternative explanations. Document null and negative results.
7. Use synthetic simulation only to generate hypotheses or test the research instrument. It is not validation. If calibration is required, compare simulations with held-out real evidence and retain separate labels.

## Output

Use the [customer research brief](../../templates/customer-research-brief.md). Include the decision rule, limits, privacy and fairness checks, and next action. Maintain the [customer evidence ledger](../../templates/customer-evidence-ledger.md) for material claims.

## Limits

Interviews do not establish prevalence; stated intent does not establish purchase; observational data alone does not establish causality; statistical significance does not establish commercial value. Do not fabricate citations, results, sample sizes, or confidence intervals. Follow the shared [evidence labels](../../references/evidence-labels.md).
