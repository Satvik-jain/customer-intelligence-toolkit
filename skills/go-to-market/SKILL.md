# Go-to-Market

Use this skill to turn customer, market, and product evidence into a staged route-to-market plan.

## Inputs

Identify product maturity, target segments, buying roles, geography, channel access, budget/team constraints, pricing, current traction, and launch decision.

## Workflow

1. Choose a beachhead segment using evidence, urgency, reachability, strategic fit, and ability to serve.
2. Match distribution and acquisition motions to buyer behavior, sales complexity, product onboarding, and channel economics.
3. Propose a small set of channel tests with audience, offer, cost, duration, measurement, and decision rule.
4. Define funnel, activation, retention, referral, acquisition cost, payback, and customer value measures that fit the business model.
5. Sequence pilot, learning, launch, and scale decisions. Include customer success and feedback loops.
6. Mark benchmarks as external reference points, not targets, unless they match the context and have traceable sources.

## Output

Provide segment, value proposition, channel/motion, staged plan, measures, assumptions, economics, risks, and next decision. See the [channel test plan](references/channel-test-plan.md) and shared [evidence labels](../../references/evidence-labels.md).

## Limits

Do not prescribe universal CAC/LTV ratios, conversion rates, or channel winners. Do not infer channel causality from correlation or scale an unvalidated motion based on synthetic simulation.
