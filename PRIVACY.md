# Privacy Policy

The Customer Intelligence & Monetization Toolkit consists of Claude Code skills, Markdown references, and output templates. Its instructions run within Claude Code using the context you choose to provide.

The plugin itself does not independently collect, store, process, or transmit personal data. It has no backend, database, analytics, tracking, telemetry, MCP servers, or external data-transmission integrations.

Information you provide during a Claude conversation is processed according to Anthropic's privacy policy and the platform terms that apply to your account and use. See Anthropic's [Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and [Legal Center](https://www.anthropic.com/legal) for applicable policies and terms.

If you independently connect external tools or services to Claude, those services have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices. Review them before use.

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information or confidential business information unless you understand the relevant platform and service policies and are authorized to provide it.
